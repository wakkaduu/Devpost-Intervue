Intervue draft

A mock interview web app that helps job applicants prepare, with AI feedback specific to each answer.

The loop at a glance

Kernel

AI writes each review, so feedback is never repetitive and gives real insight. It asks the follow-up a real interviewer would ask where your answer has holes, and a history chart shows whether you've improved since your first attempt.

Who it's for

Job applicants and interview preparers, especially people like the builder: prepared, but always nervous.

Why it matters

"I am one of those applicants that's wanted to be prepared but also always nervous."

What "working" looks like

Open the web app, pick a category, choose voice or written, answer several questions, submit. Get AI reviews, recommendations and a follow-up question, then see your progress chart. Delivery feedback = words per minute, filler words, and the AI's tone read. The chart plots each session's average score over time.
